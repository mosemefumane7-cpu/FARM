## Visual Language
- Core visual signature: crate-stencil stamping — uppercase micro-labels with wide letter-spacing inside a 1px double-rule box, used for status, packaging size and section markers.
- Material & depth: flat kraft-paper surfaces; depth comes from hairline rules and one soft shadow on raised panels, never from gradients or blur.
- Containers & buttons: cards are paper panels with a 1px border and small radius; primary buttons are solid ink-green rectangles with a 2px offset bottom edge that compresses on press; secondary actions use muted fills.
- Layout rhythm: dense ledger tables separated by generous section whitespace; accent ochre appears only on stamps, active nav markers and low-stock flags.

## Animation
- Entrance: page sections fade and rise 8px over 240ms with a 40ms stagger.
- Interaction: table rows tint to muted on hover over 120ms; buttons compress 1px on press.
- Scroll / transition: dashboard metric values count up once when they enter the viewport.

## Forbidden
- No gradients, glassmorphism or frosted overlays.
- No pill-shaped buttons or fully rounded cards.
- No decorative fruit emoji or clip-art illustrations.

## Additional Notes
- All user-visible copy is English.
- Numeric columns use tabular figures and right alignment.
